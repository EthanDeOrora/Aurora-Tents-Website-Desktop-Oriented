Our Rental Inventory

Aurora Tents and Services offers a wide range of event rental equipment to suit events of all sizes and styles. Our rentals are flexible, affordable, and designed to create comfortable and visually appealing event spaces.

Event Tents

Our event tents provide shelter, elegance, and structure for outdoor events. Available in multiple sizes and styles, our tents are suitable for weddings, corporate events, festivals, private parties, and more. Each tent is professionally installed to ensure safety, stability, and visual appeal.

Tables

We offer a variety of tables to accommodate dining, displays, gifts, and event activities. Whether you need round tables for a reception or rectangular tables for seating and catering, our tables are clean, sturdy, and ready for use.

Chairs

Comfortable seating is essential for any event. Our chairs are suitable for both casual and formal occasions and can be arranged to match your layout and event flow.

Decoration and Accessories

Enhance the look and feel of your event with our decorative options. From simple accents to custom decorative setups, we help transform ordinary spaces into memorable experiences.
